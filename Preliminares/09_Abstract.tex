  {Abstract}
  {Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (LOPDP) grants data subjects the right to be informed, requires free and informed consent, and obliges controllers to manage processing risks; however, there is no official measurement of compliance in Ecuadorian universities. The general objective is to design a risk diagnosis and management methodology for the processing of students' personal data in higher education institutions, integrating the SPDP risk management guide with ISO/IEC 27005:2022. The methodology, applicable to any university, is built on evidence from the province of Pichincha: a census of the public information of its universities and polytechnic schools (18 headquartered in the province, plus the branch campuses that are confirmed) and an anonymous survey of their adult undergraduate students. The study follows a quantitative approach, with a non-experimental, cross-sectional design and a descriptive, comparative and correlational scope; it uses no internal data from any institution and reports no results by university. The LOPDP is translated into thirty-one verifiable indicators: sixteen documentary indicators, with dated evidence and reliability measured with weighted Cohen's kappa, and fifteen perception indicators, measured with a 52-item questionnaire (version 2.1) validated through Aiken's V, a pilot test, alpha, omega, KR-20 and exploratory factor analysis. The target is 384 responses, justified by statistical power. Three hypotheses will be tested with the Mann-Whitney U test, Spearman's correlation and the chi-square test, and the gap between published and perceived information will be weighted by the composition of the sample. The six-phase methodology is validated through scenarios derived from those gaps, a calibrated Delphi panel and expert judgment; it is not applied in a real institution because access to internal data was not available. At the cut-off date (16 November 2026), the templates and scripts have been verified; the results of the census, the survey and the panel are pending and will be obtained between November 2026 and February 2027.}
  {Keywords}
  {personal data protection, regulatory compliance, risk management, transparency, university students}
