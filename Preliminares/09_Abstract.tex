  {Abstract}
  {Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (LOPDP) grants data subjects the right to be informed, requires free and informed consent when consent is the legal basis, and obliges controllers to manage processing risks; however, there is no official measurement of compliance in Ecuadorian universities. The general objective is to design a risk diagnosis and management methodology for the processing of students' personal data in higher education institutions, integrating the SPDP risk management guide with ISO/IEC 27005:2022. The methodology, applicable by any university in the country through its institutional profile, is built on national evidence: a census of the public information of the 64 universities and polytechnic schools registered with the CES, and a survey of adult undergraduate students that collects no direct identifiers. The study follows a quantitative approach, with a non-experimental, cross-sectional design and a descriptive, comparative and correlational scope; it uses no internal data from any institution and reports no results by university. The regulatory cut-off date is 16 November 2026. The LOPDP is translated into thirty-eight verifiable indicators: twenty-three documentary indicators, with dated evidence and reliability measured with weighted Cohen's kappa, and fifteen perception indicators, measured with a 55-item questionnaire (version 2.2) validated through Aiken's V, a pilot test, alpha, omega, KR-20 and exploratory factor analysis. The target is 384 responses, justified by statistical power. Three hypotheses will be tested with the Mann-Whitney U test, Spearman's correlation and the chi-square test, and the gap between published and perceived information will be weighted by the composition of the sample. The methodology comprises twelve diagnosis steps, with a maturity level based on the SPDP scale, and eleven risk management steps, with a prior screening for data protection impact assessments; it is validated through fifteen scenarios estimated for two reference profiles, a calibrated Delphi panel and expert judgment, and it comes with an application guide. It is not applied in a real institution because access to internal data was not available. At the cut-off date, the templates and scripts have been verified; the results of the census, the survey and the panel will be obtained between November 2026 and February 2027.}
  {Keywords}
